## E2

### Short

You consistently operate at moka-wide scale and make security / compliance a strategic operating system for the company.

### Scope & Autonomy

- Is a recognized expert across the company on multiple security, compliance, privacy, IT and risk topics.
- Leads highly complex, long-term initiatives with broad organizational impact.
- Operates with very high autonomy and little need for framing.
- Turns security / compliance into scalable company-wide systems, not just projects.

### Security & Compliance Planning

- Defines and continuously improves the security / compliance roadmap at company level.
- Identifies strategic priorities before they become urgent.
- Structures multi-quarter programs across Tech, Product, Legal, Sales, Care, People and Leadership.
- Aligns security priorities with business strategy, enterprise readiness, certifications, customer expectations and regulatory changes.

### Risk Analysis & Problem Solving

- Identifies systemic risks and root causes across the organization.
- Creates durable mechanisms to reduce risk over time.
- Solves ambiguous problems involving technical, organizational, legal and business trade-offs.
- Measures the maturity and effectiveness of the security program.
- Makes the organization better at anticipating risks instead of reacting to them.

### Communication & Influence

- Influences senior stakeholders and leadership on high-stakes decisions.
- Communicates complex risks in a way that drives clear decisions.
- Handles sensitive conversations with clients, auditors, partners or leadership.
- Builds strong internal and external trust.
- Makes security understandable, actionable and credible at executive level.

### Expertise

- Has deep, broad and immediately usable expertise across security governance, risk management, compliance frameworks, product security, infrastructure security, internal IT, audits, customer security, privacy and regulatory topics.
- Can represent moka externally on demanding security / compliance matters.
- Connects security expertise directly to trust, sales, resilience and company strategy.

### Knowledge Sharing & Team Enablement

- Builds repeatable systems for learning and enablement: playbooks, governance rituals, training programs, standards, internal communities and review mechanisms.
- Coaches people at multiple levels and across functions.
- Creates an organization where security ownership is distributed and not dependent on the RSSI alone.

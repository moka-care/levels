## B1

### Short

You master some RSSI tools and processes, but are not fully autonomous on complex topics yet.

### Scope & Autonomy

- Is a reliable contributor for the team.
- Owns small RSSI projects impacting the direct team.
- Develops technical, security and business skills.
- Follows the strategy and objectives set by the team / coach / mentors.
- Can handle simple topics end-to-end with validation on complex cases.

### Security & Compliance Planning

- Plans and manages daily work without getting lost in details.
- Decides own tasks within a given project.
- Takes feedback into account when making decisions.
- Makes progress visible and raises delays, dependencies or risks.

### Risk Analysis & Problem Solving

- Brings new ideas and constructively challenges existing processes or proposals.
- Spots problems and suggests relevant solutions.
- Starts connecting security issues to business stakes.
- Distinguishes theoretical, real, acceptable and action-requiring risks.

### Communication & Influence

- Communicates clearly and concisely.
- Shares important information proactively.
- Builds relationships across the company.
- Explains security / compliance topics to non-experts.
- Makes security topics less unclear for teams.

### Expertise

- Has first applicable experience in the role.
- Understands the challenges of joining an early-stage company.
- Masters some internal tools and processes.
- Is effective on known topics, with support for complex cases.

### Knowledge Sharing & Team Enablement

- Takes feedback into account.
- Shares feedback on own work.
- Reports daily work in writing.
- Can act as an onboarding buddy.
- Documents simple procedures they own.

## B1

### Short

You master some RSSI tools and processes, but are not fully autonomous on complex topics yet.

### Scope & Autonomy

- Owns small RSSI projects impacting the direct team, and handles simple topics end-to-end with validation on complex cases.
- Follows the strategy and objectives set by the team / coach / mentors.

### Security & Compliance Planning

- Decides own tasks within a given project, and makes progress, delays and dependencies visible.

### Risk Analysis & Problem Solving

- Constructively challenges existing processes or proposals, and spots problems with relevant solutions.
- Distinguishes theoretical, real, acceptable and action-requiring risks.

### Communication & Influence

- Explains security / compliance topics to non-experts, and builds relationships beyond the direct team.

### Expertise

- Has first applicable experience in the role, and masters some internal tools and processes.

### Knowledge Sharing & Team Enablement

- Reports daily work in writing, and documents simple procedures they own.
- Can act as an onboarding buddy.

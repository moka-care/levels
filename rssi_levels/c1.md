## C1

### Short

You are fully autonomous in your RSSI role and start helping others grow.

### Scope & Autonomy

- Proactively participates in solving problems for the team.
- Owns small and medium projects independently.
- Can take any challenge within scope confidently.
- Owns several recurring RSSI areas: customer compliance, internal IT, monitoring / alerting, security policies, audits, access management, awareness.

### Security & Compliance Planning

- Decides which projects to work on.
- Gathers missing information.
- Aligns with leadership on objectives and projects.
- Makes relevant information accessible.
- Structures priorities based on risk, business impact, effort, deadlines and team maturity.

### Risk Analysis & Problem Solving

- Implements solutions autonomously.
- Consistently brings identified problems with potential solutions.
- Breaks down security / compliance issues into concrete action plans.
- Anticipates risks on product / tech projects.
- Balances security rigor with business pragmatism.

### Communication & Influence

- Shares the right information with the right people at the right time.
- Has strong relationships across the company.
- Translates complex topics into clear messages.
- Raises the security understanding of teams.
- Is seen as a reliable point of contact.

### Expertise

- Has several years of applicable experience or equivalent.
- Brings strong value through security expertise and soft skills.
- Masters the main RSSI topics in moka's context.
- Uses tools, frameworks and processes without constant supervision.

### Knowledge Sharing & Team Enablement

- Documents work regularly.
- Makes learnings accessible.
- Mentors more junior or less expert people on security / compliance topics.
- Shares proactive constructive feedback.
- Creates support materials: compliance docs, IT guides, customer response templates, incident procedures, audit checklists.

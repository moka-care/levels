## C1

### Short

You are fully autonomous in your RSSI role and start helping others grow.

### Scope & Autonomy

- Owns several recurring RSSI areas independently: customer compliance, internal IT, monitoring / alerting, security policies, audits, access management, awareness.
- Can take any challenge within scope confidently.

### Security & Compliance Planning

- Decides which projects to work on, and aligns with leadership on objectives.
- Organizes task planning by priority, based on risk, business impact, effort, deadlines and team maturity.

### Risk Analysis & Problem Solving

- Breaks down security / compliance issues into concrete action plans.
- Anticipates risks on product / tech projects, balancing security rigor with business pragmatism.

### Communication & Influence

- Translates complex topics into clear messages, and collaborates directly with other teams as their point of contact on security topics.

### Expertise

- Masters the main RSSI topics in moka's context, and uses tools, frameworks and processes without constant supervision.

### Knowledge Sharing & Team Enablement

- Mentors more junior or less expert people on security / compliance topics.
- Creates support materials: compliance docs, IT guides, customer response templates, incident procedures, audit checklists.

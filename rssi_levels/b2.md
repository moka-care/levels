## B2

### Short

You can own recurring RSSI topics, understand security / business impacts, and start structuring your scope.

### Scope & Autonomy

- Owns small to medium projects impacting the direct team.
- Can take most challenges within their scope confidently.
- Starts becoming the natural point of contact on recurring topics: internal IT, standard security questionnaires, access reviews, compliance documentation, awareness, remediation follow-up.

### Security & Compliance Planning

- Decides what to work on within a defined frame.
- Proactively gathers missing information.
- Validates team alignment.
- Makes information accessible.
- Structures next steps for simple or medium-complexity topics.

### Risk Analysis & Problem Solving

- Implements solutions autonomously, whether they originated from them or not.
- Regularly brings spotted problems with potential solutions.
- Investigates security issues up to a decision proposal.
- Starts measuring the effectiveness of actions.
- Knows when to escalate.

### Communication & Influence

- Shares the right level of information with the right people at the right time.
- Has built relationships across the company.
- Explains security trade-offs clearly.
- Can make simple security constraints accepted by explaining their value.

### Expertise

- Has several years of applicable experience, or equivalent responsibilities.
- Brings direct value through security / technical skills and soft skills.
- Understands the main applicable frameworks and obligations: RGPD, ISO 27001, HDS, product security, IT security, customer requirements.

### Knowledge Sharing & Team Enablement

- Documents work regularly.
- Makes learnings reusable by the team.
- Can mentor more junior people on simple topics.
- Shares respectful and constructive feedback.
- Produces reusable assets: procedures, checklists, response templates, internal guides.

## B2

### Short

You can own recurring RSSI topics and medium projects, you understand security / business impacts and start structuring your scope.

### Scope & Autonomy

- Owns small and medium projects, and takes most challenges within their scope confidently.
- Is the natural point of contact on recurring topics: internal IT, standard security questionnaires, access reviews, compliance documentation, awareness, remediation follow-up.

### Security & Compliance Planning

- Decides what to work on within a defined frame, and structures next steps on medium-complexity topics.

### Risk Analysis & Problem Solving

- Investigates security issues up to a decision proposal, and knows when to escalate.
- Implements solutions autonomously, whether they originated from them or not, and starts measuring their effectiveness.

### Communication & Influence

- Explains security trade-offs clearly, and gets simple constraints accepted by explaining their value.

### Expertise

- Understands the main applicable frameworks and obligations: RGPD, ISO 27001, HDS, product security, IT security, customer requirements.

### Knowledge Sharing & Team Enablement

- Produces reusable assets: procedures, checklists, response templates, internal guides.

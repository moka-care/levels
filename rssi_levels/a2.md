## A2

### Short

You understand the system better and contribute reliably to simple RSSI topics within a clear frame.

### Scope & Autonomy

- Handles simple security / compliance tasks with clear guidance: collecting information for security questionnaires, basic access reviews, documentation updates, remediation follow-up, preparation of awareness material.
- Asks for help at the right time.
- Gains autonomy on repeatable tasks.

### Security & Compliance Planning

- Organizes tasks and follows commitments.
- Tracks progress.
- Identifies missing information.
- Starts prioritizing based on risk, business impact, customer / audit deadlines and team capacity.

### Risk Analysis & Problem Solving

- Identifies inconsistencies or weak signals on simple topics.
- Suggests remediation options.
- Starts understanding the impact of security issues on users, customers, internal teams and compliance.
- Can frame a problem clearly.

### Communication & Influence

- Shares important information proactively.
- Explains basic security topics simply.
- Builds trust with the direct team.
- Starts adapting the level of detail to the audience.

### Expertise

- Develops an initial understanding of moka's data, internal tools, security / IT support channels, RGPD / ISO / HDS obligations and customer response workflows.
- Contributes efficiently on known topics.

### Knowledge Sharing & Team Enablement

- Updates existing documentation.
- Formalizes learnings.
- Incorporates feedback into work.
- Can help a newcomer on simple topics already mastered.

## D1

### Short

You actively raise moka's security, compliance and operational maturity, with sustainability in mind.

### Scope & Autonomy

- Is an expert on several RSSI topics that teams naturally rely on.
- Focuses on process optimization, knowledge sharing, security system structuring and team autonomy.
- Leads complex, impactful short and medium term projects with very little external input.
- Has clear impact across several teams.

### Security & Compliance Planning

- Defines project scope and direction clearly.
- Breaks projects into reachable milestones and timelines.
- Participates in team roadmap and OKR definition.
- Prioritizes security / compliance work based on risk, business impact, customer exposure, audit deadlines and team capacity.

### Risk Analysis & Problem Solving

- Measures the outcomes of implemented solutions and optimizes them afterwards.
- Reduces complexity through automation, standardization, documentation, tooling and innovation.
- Proactively identifies and addresses complex issues through a strong understanding of business stakes.
- Turns vague or stressful topics into clear action plans.

### Communication & Influence

- Synthesizes diverse inputs into clear actions or learnings.
- Has an excellent internal network and starts building an external network with peers, auditors, providers, security communities, customers or prospects.
- Is clearly perceived as an expert internally, including outside Tech.
- Secures buy-in before structural changes.

### Expertise

- Has recognized, immediately applicable expertise.
- Accelerates the team through expertise.
- Challenges existing processes, methods and orientations.
- Masters several domains: security governance, risk management, regulatory compliance, product security, infrastructure security, internal IT, audits, customer responses.

### Knowledge Sharing & Team Enablement

- Is an effective partner with any stakeholder.
- Ensures team alignment in project delivery.
- Gives feedback on other teams' work.
- Produces documented feedback reusable as a model.
- Coaches / mentors people on security and compliance.
- Creates tools and processes that make teams more autonomous.

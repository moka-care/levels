## E1

### Short

You identify and solve complex technical and organizational issues at moka scale.

### Scope & Autonomy

- Is an expert on several topics across the department or company.
- Leads complex and challenging long-term projects.
- Consistently delivers against ambitious goals.
- Influences moka's security / compliance trajectory.
- Defines standards beyond direct scope.
- Structures the organization to make security sustainable and scalable.

### Security & Compliance Planning

- Identifies new priorities that help the company move faster and more effectively.
- Structures teams or working groups to deliver them.
- Is an essential contributor to security strategy, OKRs and departmental roadmap.
- Anticipates future needs: certifications, enterprise markets, regulatory requirements, new products, international expansion, AI / sensitive data.

### Risk Analysis & Problem Solving

- Proposes solutions outside their own work context.
- Positively impacts other teams' work.
- Influences the role of security / compliance at moka.
- Identifies major issues, creates improvement plans and influences the whole company around them.
- Has a global understanding of the business and organization.
- Arbitrates strategically between risk, growth, compliance and velocity.

### Communication & Influence

- Manages expectations of key stakeholders.
- Handles discussions involving high risk or complexity.
- Listens and guides discussions to help teams reach their goals.
- Communicates decisions and rationale clearly.
- Is recognized internally as an expert on multiple topics, including outside pure RSSI scope.
- Has a strong internal and external network and uses it for strategic decisions.

### Expertise

- Has strong immediately usable expertise.
- Frames new, uncertain or poorly documented topics.
- Can represent moka on complex security / compliance matters.
- Connects architecture, product, data, legal, compliance, operations and business strategy.
- Positions security as a trust and growth lever.

### Knowledge Sharing & Team Enablement

- Facilitates and optimizes cross-team / cross-functional collaboration.
- Dedicates clear time to team upskilling.
- Coaches / mentors people at all levels.
- Creates learning systems: training, standards, playbooks, reviews, internal communities, governance.
- Makes the organization less dependent on one person for RSSI topics.

## E1

### Short

You identify and solve complex technical and organizational issues at moka scale.

### Scope & Autonomy

- Is an expert on several topics across the department or company, and leads complex long-term projects.
- Defines standards beyond their direct scope.

### Security & Compliance Planning

- Is an essential contributor to security strategy, OKRs and departmental roadmap.
- Anticipates future needs: certifications, enterprise markets, regulatory requirements, new products, international expansion, AI / sensitive data.

### Risk Analysis & Problem Solving

- Identifies major issues, creates improvement plans and influences the whole company around them.
- Arbitrates strategically between risk, growth, compliance and velocity.

### Communication & Influence

- Handles discussions involving high risk or complexity, and communicates decisions and their rationale clearly.

### Expertise

- Frames new, uncertain or poorly documented topics, and positions security as a trust and growth lever.

### Knowledge Sharing & Team Enablement

- Creates learning systems: training, standards, playbooks, reviews, internal communities, governance.

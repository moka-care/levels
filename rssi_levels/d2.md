## D2

### Short

You make the organization durably better on security and compliance beyond your personal execution.

### Scope & Autonomy

- Is a transverse reference on several RSSI topics.
- Leads complex projects involving multiple teams.
- Builds systems instead of only handling requests.
- Makes security / compliance scalable.
- Raises the bar without blocking execution.
- Delivers reliably on ambitious and visible goals.

### Security & Compliance Planning

- Structures multi-team initiatives.
- Clarifies responsibilities, milestones and success criteria.
- Actively contributes to the security / compliance roadmap.
- Influences product / tech roadmap when risks or obligations require it.
- Prioritizes maturely between compliance, risk reduction, time-to-market, customer experience and operational capacity.

### Risk Analysis & Problem Solving

- Identifies complex problems before they become blockers.
- Builds prevention mechanisms.
- Optimizes solutions after deployment.
- Measures control effectiveness.
- Reduces dependency on individual expertise through processes, tools and standards.
- Addresses root issues: governance, security debt, documentation debt, control coverage, team maturity.

### Communication & Influence

- Turns multiple, sometimes conflicting inputs into clear decisions.
- Manages stakeholders with different priorities: Tech, Product, Sales, Customer Care, Legal / DPO, Leadership, customers, auditors.
- Communicates risks in an actionable way.
- Can say no or set guardrails without breaking execution momentum.

### Expertise

- Challenges team and company orientations through deep expertise.
- Handles ambiguous topics with little framing.
- Connects security expertise to business impact.
- Quickly evaluates risk level, applicable obligations, required controls, effort level and acceptable trade-offs.

### Knowledge Sharing & Team Enablement

- Actively coaches / mentors people on security and compliance.
- Structures rituals, documentation or training that spread security culture.
- Makes feedback reusable.
- Helps other teams integrate security into their own workflows.
- Creates a dynamic where security is shared, not only owned by the RSSI.

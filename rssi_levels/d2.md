## D2

### Short

You make the organization durably better on security and compliance beyond your personal execution.

### Scope & Autonomy

- Builds systems instead of handling requests, making security / compliance scalable.

### Security & Compliance Planning

- Structures multi-team initiatives with clear responsibilities, milestones and success criteria.
- Prioritizes maturely between compliance, risk reduction, time-to-market, customer experience and operational capacity, and influences the product / tech roadmap when risks or obligations require it.

### Risk Analysis & Problem Solving

- Identifies complex problems before they become blockers, and builds prevention mechanisms.
- Addresses root issues: governance, security debt, documentation debt, control coverage, team maturity.

### Communication & Influence

- Manages stakeholders with different priorities: Tech, Product, Sales, Customer Care, Legal / DPO, Leadership, customers, auditors.
- Can say no or set guardrails without breaking execution momentum.
- Starts building an external network: peers, auditors, providers, security communities, customers or prospects.

### Expertise

- Handles ambiguous topics with little framing, and quickly evaluates risk level, applicable obligations, required controls and acceptable trade-offs.

### Knowledge Sharing & Team Enablement

- Reduces the organization's dependency on individual expertise through processes, tools and standards.

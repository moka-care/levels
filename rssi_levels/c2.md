## C2

### Short

You are autonomous, structure your RSSI scope, and start making the organization grow sustainably.

### Scope & Autonomy

- Decides which projects to work on, and aligns with leadership on objectives.
- Identifies useful RSSI hats, and makes some of them transferable to the team.

### Security & Compliance Planning

- Stays ahead of the tech / product team: fills the compliance section of the tech spec template before implementation starts.
- Plans RSSI initiatives over several weeks / cycles, and prepares teams for audits, customer responses, certifications and product / infra changes.

### Risk Analysis & Problem Solving

- Highlights prerequisites and risks, current or potential, associated with a new feature.
- Decomposes a technical or business issue into a solution spanning several Tech PM, Cooldown or Cycle, and challenges technical / product decisions when security or compliance is involved.

### Communication & Influence

- Gets teams onboard with topics that may feel restrictive, and answers customers and stakeholders without overpromising.
- Principal entry point for legal questions (Smash)

### Expertise

- Writes scripts and tests that detect security issues.
- Uses Compliance Ops tools (Bastion) to identify security issues and requirements missing against the compliance framework (CNIL / RGPD / ISO / HDS).

### Knowledge Sharing & Team Enablement

- Runs presentations or awareness sessions
- Mentors less expert people on security / compliance topics.

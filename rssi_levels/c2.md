## C2

### Short

You are autonomous, structure your RSSI scope, and start making the organization grow sustainably.

### Scope & Autonomy

- Identifies useful RSSI hats, and makes some of them transferable to the team.
- Leads several areas and ensures the processes are actually adopted.

### Security & Compliance Planning

- Plans RSSI initiatives over several weeks / cycles, and prepares teams for audits, customer responses, certifications and product / infra changes.

### Risk Analysis & Problem Solving

- Decomposes a technical or business issue into a solution spanning several Tech PM, Cooldown or Cycle.
- Challenges technical / product decisions when security or compliance is involved, and proposes controls: security CI, alerting, tests, Bastion, documented reviews.

### Communication & Influence

- Gets teams onboard with topics that may feel restrictive, and answers customers and stakeholders without overpromising.

### Expertise

- Navigates several layers of the RSSI stack (Bastion, CNIL / RGPD / ISO / HDS, Datadog security alerts, admin access, internal processes) and uses them to detect gaps and weaknesses.

### Knowledge Sharing & Team Enablement

- Runs presentations or awareness sessions, and looks for mentoring or coaching opportunities.

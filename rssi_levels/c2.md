## C2

### Short

You are autonomous, structure your RSSI scope, and start making the organization grow sustainably.

### Scope & Autonomy

- Identifies useful RSSI hats.
- Leads several areas: security, IT, monitoring / alerting, customer compliance, certification readiness, documentation, awareness.
- Optimizes processes and ensures adoption.
- Handles transverse topics with little external input.
- Starts making some hats transferable to the team.

### Security & Compliance Planning

- Plans RSSI initiatives over several weeks / cycles.
- Proactively gathers missing information before deciding.
- Validates alignment with team and stakeholders.
- Prepares teams for important deadlines: audits, customer responses, certifications, product / infra changes.

### Risk Analysis & Problem Solving

- Breaks down technical or business issues into solutions across several Tech PMs, cooldowns or cycles.
- Challenges technical / product decisions when security or compliance is involved.
- Highlights prerequisites, current risks, future risks and uncertainty.
- Proposes controls: security CI, alerting, tests, Bastion, documented reviews.

### Communication & Influence

- Explains constraints pedagogically.
- Gets teams onboard with topics that may feel restrictive.
- Answers customers and stakeholders clearly.
- Reassures without overpromising.
- Starts turning security into a shared reflex.

### Expertise

- Masters several layers: Bastion, CNIL / RGPD / ISO / HDS frameworks, Datadog / security alerts, admin access, internal processes, IT, customer compliance.
- Uses these to detect gaps, risks and weaknesses.
- Becomes a strong reference on several RSSI topics.

### Knowledge Sharing & Team Enablement

- Looks for mentoring / coaching opportunities.
- Runs presentations or awareness sessions.
- Structures and updates documentation.
- Creates training or onboarding material.
- Gives useful feedback on security practices.

## A1

### Short

You are starting to learn how moka's security, compliance, IT and product systems work.

### Scope & Autonomy

- Supports the team on clearly scoped RSSI tasks, with regular support to prioritize and validate actions.
- Discovers the main areas of the role: product security, internal IT, compliance, access management, customer security requests, documentation.

### Security & Compliance Planning

- Manages assigned tasks and follows priorities defined by the team.
- Raises blockers, missing information and uncertainty early.

### Risk Analysis & Problem Solving

- Asks questions that clarify risks, and suggests early solutions that still need validation.

### Communication & Influence

- Reports progress and difficulties proactively to the direct team.

### Expertise

- Is learning the role, tools, internal processes and applicable security / compliance requirements.

### Knowledge Sharing & Team Enablement

- Asks for feedback and acts on it, and documents discoveries to support their own onboarding.

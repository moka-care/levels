## A1

### Short

You are starting to learn how moka's security, compliance, IT and product systems work.

### Scope & Autonomy

- Supports the team on clearly scoped RSSI tasks.
- Discovers the main areas of the role: product security, internal IT, compliance, access management, customer security requests, documentation.
- Needs regular support to prioritize, decide and validate actions.

### Security & Compliance Planning

- Manages time and assigned tasks effectively.
- Follows priorities defined by the team.
- Raises blockers, missing information and uncertainty early.
- Starts learning how to distinguish urgent issues from important-but-not-urgent topics.

### Risk Analysis & Problem Solving

- Brings a fresh perspective.
- Asks useful questions to clarify risks.
- Helps the team see issues from different angles.
- Suggests early solutions that still need validation.

### Communication & Influence

- Reports progress and difficulties proactively.
- Communicates clearly and concisely.
- Shares important information with the direct team.
- Starts building trust with close collaborators.

### Expertise

- Is learning the role, tools, internal processes, moka context and applicable security / compliance requirements.
- Brings value mostly through rigor, curiosity, reliability and good questions.

### Knowledge Sharing & Team Enablement

- Receives feedback and acts on it.
- Asks for feedback.
- Shares learnings mostly in 1:1s.
- Starts documenting discoveries to support their own onboarding.
